# Validation Report: Official Qwixx Score-pad Variants

**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Mode:** Goal-based
**Status:** PASSED

## Goal

Research additional official Qwixx variants and complete the playable digital score-pad presets.

## Criteria and evidence

1. **Official sources collected:** NSV rules were checked for Double, Bonus, Connected, X-Change, and On Board; the research document records the sources and scope decisions.
2. **Compatible score-pad variants are playable:** Double A/B, Bonus A/B, Connected A/B, and X-Change are available beside Classic, gemixxt A/B, Longo, and Big Points.
3. **Variant mechanics are enforced:** second crosses, multiplier cells, chained mandatory marks, symbol rewards, Connected scoring/links, and ordered sum exchanges are covered by engine tests.
4. **AI and observation/action encoding remain usable:** all new action families round-trip through the codec and random AI games terminate on all presets.
5. **UI exposes and explains the variants:** setup cards, special-cell badges, progress badges, mandatory-bonus phase text, scoring, and player manual were updated.
6. **Different-component products are not misrepresented as score pads:** On Board, the card game, and Duell remain documented research rather than ordinary-dice presets.

## Qualification

Connected's official A–E mechanics and per-player sheet structure are implemented, but its precise printed A–E cell layouts are transparent digital reconstructions because NSV does not publish machine-readable sheet data. Double, Bonus, and X-Change special positions were checked against official rules/card imagery.
